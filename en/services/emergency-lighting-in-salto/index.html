Completed work · Artigas

Completed installation at a branch in Artigas.

Nine self-contained emergency lights were installed: five on the ground floor, two on the mezzanine and two on the upper floor. The solution was adapted during execution to the building’s construction characteristics.

The project included three supply circuits, approximately 60 to 65 metres of surface-mounted galvanized steel conduit and between 270 and 285 metres of conductors. An eight-module auxiliary panel contains 25 A / 30 mA residual-current protection and two C10 circuit breakers: one supplies circuits 1 and 2 with five lights, and the other supplies circuit 3 with four lights.
